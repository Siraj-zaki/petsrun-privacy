🐾 Pets Run

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026 · App: Pets Run (Android, package com.petsrun.game)

Summary

Pets Run is a game for kids and grown-ups. We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no chat, no in-app purchases and no analytics. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows family-safe ads from Google AdMob, which are set up so that they are never personalised and never use your advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, best score, unlocked pets and worlds, power-up levels) and your settings (music, sound, vibration, graphics) in a file on your device. This file is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Pets Run free, the game sometimes shows a full-screen ad between runs, never during play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. We configure every ad request so that:

  • it is tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR), so ads are non-personalised and contain no interest-based targeting or remarketing;
  • only G-rated (suitable for all ages) ads are allowed;
  • the Android advertising ID is not collected. The game does not request that permission.

To deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process limited technical information:

DataPurpose
IP address (used to estimate a general location, e.g. country)Ad delivery, fraud prevention
Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, app-set ID)Ad delivery, frequency capping, analytics for the ad service
Ad interactions (e.g. whether an ad was shown or tapped)Ad measurement, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (crash logs and performance data of the ads SDK)Keeping the ads service reliable

This data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

Pets Run is designed to be safe for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Links that leave the game (like this privacy policy) are behind a parental gate: a grown-up maths question. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only to load ads. The game itself works fully offline.
  • Vibration: short haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.

Your choices

  • Turn off internet access (e.g. airplane mode) to play without ads loading.
  • Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland), an Ad Consent button in Settings lets you review your choices.
  • Uninstall the game at any time to delete all locally stored data.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone. Apart from the ad requests described above, the game sends no data anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above.
